03 Software

Orpheus

Python · Tkinter · Three.js · Objective-C · about 27,000 lines

A songwriting workshop, from lyrics to chords. A vibe-coded project: designed and directed by me, code written in conversation with Claude Code. Born on Windows, later ported to Mac.

It counts sung syllables, not poetic feet, and checks that the lyrics can be sung on the melody.

A chord map in 2D and 3D: you compose by clicking from one chord to the next, and every progression can be heard.

It finds the chords of any song, MIDI or MP3, with audio AI running locally (Demucs, Beat This!, BTC, CREPE).

It talks to a MIDI keyboard and to Logic Pro, through a home-made Audio Unit plugin.
